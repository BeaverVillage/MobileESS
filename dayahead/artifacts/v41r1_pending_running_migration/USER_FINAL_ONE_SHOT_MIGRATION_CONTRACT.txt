# 7. RUNNING MIGRATION — EXACTLY ONE OPPORTUNITY

# 9. ONE-SHOT DECISION AT THAT CHECKPOINT

At FIRST_VALID_CHECKPOINT_j the Day-Ahead optimizer chooses exactly one
of:

A) STAY at the current IDC

or

B) MIGRATE once to one feasible destination IDC


Conceptually:

migrate_j in {0,1}


If:

migrate_j = 0

then the job remains at its current IDC for the rest of the Day-D
modeled horizon.


It does NOT receive another migration opportunity at a later checkpoint.


If:

migrate_j = 1

then it performs one checkpoint-based migration and cannot migrate
again during Day D.
