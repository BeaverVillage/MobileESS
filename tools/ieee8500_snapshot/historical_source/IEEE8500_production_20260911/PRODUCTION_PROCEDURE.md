# IEEE8500 production procedure

The FINAL operating point is 2025-05-21, source 1.0400 pu, every regulator Vreg 123.5 V, alpha 0.50, CAPBank3 OFF. All native topology, impedance, ratings, controlled capacitor logic, 12 AIDC/24 MESS PCCs, host mapping, GPU/power/energy scales and hard limits remain immutable. This is the previously declared compatibility adaptation, not canonical-case reproduction.

Preflight must pass before optimization. It requires exact B0 96-slot reproduction, the complete non-electrical candidate and service/PCC binding audit, and independent signed P/Q perturbation validation. Version 1's failed magnitude derivatives at zero MESS injection are preserved. Version 2 uses signed complex voltage/current and winding P+jQ derivatives, with magnitudes formed after summation. Both versions and validation evidence remain visible. All new electrical coefficients originate from IEEE8500; no IEEE123 electrical artifact is loaded. Scalar bus/element AC validation is separate from bulk derivative measurement.

The full 1,341,947-option, 708-job V41R4 non-electrical domain is retained. The IEEE8500 implementation is a bounded joint-neighborhood matheuristic; it does not claim an identical solver matrix or global optimality. B1 and B3-A1 use the same full domain, frozen B0 service/reference authority, GPU/C1 power tables, gang/rack capacity, D-1 H4 reserve, deterministic first-checkpoint/UID-serial fixed-path full-rate WAN and restart semantics. Continuous search rotates neighborhoods of 8/12/16 jobs and ranked batches of 64/128/256 options, plus every transfer-start band, rotating options and the incumbent. No option is permanently removed. Ranking uses only the new IEEE8500 coefficients and current policy state. P1 is primary; every fifth neighborhood searches P2 under a P1 lock. Acceptance uses the exact AC P1, then H4 reserve shortfall, migrations, full reference occupancy deviation, and deterministic per-UID option ordinal. P1 cannot be degraded to improve lower priorities. This IEEE8500 stable tie is explicitly scoped to this new implementation.

B1 and B3-A1 each receive a single 14,400-second continuous search-loop deadline, starting after initial model inputs and exact seed validation. Build, ranking, LP/MILP, separation and validation inside the loop consume that clock. There is no separate 30-minute run, objective-floor stop or stagnation stop. A separate checkpoint observer records the best already independently validated feasible incumbent at 1,800/3,600/7,200/14,400 seconds. Unfinished proposals do not replace it. Final independent replay and serialization occur outside the search-loop budget and are included in total runtime. A final minute without starting a new long AC replay still continues candidate/MILP search, preserving the latest accepted incumbent at the deadline.

B2 fixes B0 AIDC and optimizes MESS. B3 reuses the exact final B1 as A0; M1 searches the full 4-MESS joint route domain over all 24 locations and 96 departure slots, then A1 reoptimizes the same full AIDC domain with M1 route/location/P/Q fixed, and MF optimizes only P/Q with M1 routes fixed. Road service IDs IDC01–IDC12 are aliases of electrical AIDC01–AIDC12; no road geometry, travel time or energy is changed.

The full MESS search includes every route arc whose connection-ready arrival is within the horizon, without a top-K route restriction. Full-route MILP uses 600 seconds in its first solve and 180 seconds in subsequent separation solves; the predeclared work limits are 60/180/300 (then 300), with at most 8 solves. MF has only fixed-route P/Q, direction and battery variables, at most 8 solves of 180 seconds. These are bounded searches, not optimality certificates. Original 300-kW/400-kVA/1200-kWh units, 440–1080-kWh bounds, initial/terminal 760 kWh, efficiencies 0.95 and the original 16-face PCS constraint are preserved.

Grid models add all-axis separating constraints on signed phasor-affine voltage, line phase current, transformer phase current and winding P+jQ norms. Every proposed solution is checked against every physical axis. Final acceptance always requires fresh chronological exact OpenDSS AC with native production solver/control settings. For MESS only, an AC-rejected proposal may supply a recorded numerical magnitude-defect correction to the surrogate constants for the next bounded solve; this never changes a physical parameter, hard limit or FINAL authority. Each policy starts from the original validated IEEE8500 coefficient version. The best exact feasible seed is retained if the bounded search finds no better validated solution.

All final B0/B1/B2/B3 solutions require an additional clean 96-slot replay. Metrics distinguish coefficient generation, candidate ranking, total candidate evaluation throughput, LP/MILP solves, exact AC validations, incumbent updates, checkpoints, time to best P1, M1/MF and policy runtime, and sampled aggregate process RAM. No May B1/B2/B3 performance or Actual inputs are permitted.
